Four rules that apply to every frame

Carried over from the dealer comment standard. They matter more on video, because overlays get screenshotted and reshared without the listing attached.

  1. No prices, no MSRP, no payments, any lane. A screenshot of a price outlives the price.
  2. No vehicle history on screen. No owner counts, no accident language, no Carfax stills. The linked report carries all of it.
  3. No mileage unless the number itself is the story, and only when it is verified in HomeNet. 93,862 on a 1994 earns a frame. 41,200 on a 2022 does not.
  4. Three chips maximum in any one frame, six across an entire video. Over the ceiling means you are listing. Cut specs, never voice.
