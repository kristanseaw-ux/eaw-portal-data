Project brief

Create a branded lower-third banner overlay for social-media video, using the fonts and visual language of the
EAW Daily Totals site. The design evolved into a 4:3 layout with the official EAW logo, dealership phone number,
and the tagline: SEAMLESS. STRESS FREE. BUILT ON RELATIONSHIPS.
