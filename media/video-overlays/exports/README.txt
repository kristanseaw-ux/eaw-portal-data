PICK ONE TEXTURE
01 and 02 are the same layout in the two approved textures. Choose one
and use it on everything. Do not mix them across a batch of videos.

ABOUT THE BLUR
Four templates were designed with a blur behind the panel: 04 glass slab,
07 fade column, 09 corner wedge, and 13 lower third. A PNG cannot blur
what sits behind it, so these exported as flat semi-transparent panels.
They work as they are. If you want the blur, add a blurred mask in the
video editor once per template and reuse it.

RULES FOR ANY FRAME
1. No prices, no MSRP, no payments.
2. No vehicle history on screen.
3. No mileage unless the number itself is the story, verified in HomeNet.
4. Three spec chips maximum in one frame, six across a whole video.
